| Column     | Meaning                                                                                                               |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| TEAM       | School name                                                                                                           |
| CONF       | Conference abbreviation — the league the school plays in                                                              |
| G          | Games played                                                                                                          |
| W          | Games won                                                                                                             |
| ADJOE      | Points they'd score per 100 possessions vs an average D-I defense (higher is better)                                  |
| ADJDE      | Points they'd allow per 100 possessions vs an average D-I offense (lower is better)                                   |
| BARTHAG    | Chance of beating an average D-I team, 0 to 1                                                                         |
| WAB        | Wins above bubble — how many more games they won than a tournament cut-line team would have against the same schedule |
| ADJ_T      | Pace: possessions per 40 minutes vs an average-tempo opponent                                                         |
| EFG_O      | Their own effective field goal % (threes counted as 1.5 shots)                                                        |
| EFG_D      | Effective field goal % they allowed opponents                                                                         |
| 2P_O       | Two-point shooting % made                                                                                             |
| 2P_D       | Two-point shooting % allowed                                                                                          |
| 3P_O       | Three-point shooting % made                                                                                           |
| 3P_D       | Three-point shooting % allowed                                                                                        |
| TOR        | Share of their own possessions lost to a turnover                                                                     |
| TORD       | Share of opponent possessions they force into a turnover                                                              |
| ORB        | Share of their own missed shots they rebound                                                                          |
| DRB        | Share of their missed shots the opponent rebounds                                                                     |
| FTR        | How often they reach the free throw line, per 100 field goal attempts                                                 |
| FTRD       | How often opponents reach the free throw line against them                                                            |
| SEED       | Tournament seed, 1 (best) to 16 — blank for non-tournament teams                                                      |
| POSTSEASON | Round where their season ended — blank for non-tournament teams                                                       |
